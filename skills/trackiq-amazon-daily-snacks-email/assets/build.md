# Producing one send

## 1. Copy and fill
`cp assets/template.html snacks-<YYYY-MM-DD>.html`, copy both logo files
into a sibling `assets/`, and fill `{{HEADLINE}}`, `{{DATE_UPPER}}`,
`{{BRAND_UPPER}}`, `{{ACCOUNT}}`, `{{MARKET}}`.

## 2. Find the story before you touch the numbers
Snacks needs a thesis. Compute marginal return per line —
`Δretail ÷ Δspend` — and the story is almost always the spread between
the best and worst line. That comparison is not in any report; it is the
whole reason this email exists.

## 3. Fill the sections in this order
Numbers → line rankings → charts → shelf → quick bites → Big Bite →
Takeaway → Snack Fact → menu → opener. Prose last, because prose makes
claims about tables.

## 4. Derived values that must tie
- Retail per ad dollar = line retail ÷ line spend
- Marginal ad ACoS = Δspend ÷ Δretail
- Revenue mix halves sum to week retail
- Every summary tile is COUNTED from the cards below it, never estimated
- TACoS moves in `pp`; everything else in `%`

## 5. Weekday labels
Get the calendar right. Windows are Sun–Sat. Verify the actual weekday of
each date before labelling the daily bars, and never call a day
"yesterday" unless the send date makes that literally true — the settled
day is usually two or three days back.

## 6. De-duplicate
Read the finished email top to bottom and delete any number stated twice.
Adding a chart usually orphans a quick bite; adding a shelf card usually
orphans a bullet. This is the most common defect in this format.

## 7. Ship checks
- No platform named but TrackIQ and Amazon
- Every aggregate agrees with its detail
- Every chart bar proportional; label columns clear of bars
- No text under 12px except uppercase eyebrows
- No unsubscribe, preferences, or postal address
- Under 100KB; logo absolute https
